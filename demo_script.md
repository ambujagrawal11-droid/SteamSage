# Three-Minute Demo Video Script

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem
“Factories can operate hundreds of steam traps. A missed leak can waste steam, while a blocked trap can cause condensate and process problems. Maintenance teams need a way to turn inspection records into a prioritized action list.”

## 0:20–0:40 — Product
“This is SteamSage, an explainable steam-trap diagnostic and maintenance-prioritization prototype for the Factories, Fleets & Supply Chains track.”

## 0:40–1:10 — Exasol workflow
“SteamSage loads 500 reproducible synthetic inspection records into Exasol Personal. SQL classifies records using transparent demo rules and ranks inspection priorities. Exasol is used for storage and query execution, not just mentioned in the slide deck.”

## 1:10–1:50 — Dashboard
“Here are the number of traps analyzed, records flagged for review, high-priority checks, and an illustrative annual cost exposure. The table can be filtered by priority and exported for a maintenance review.”

## 1:50–2:25 — Explain a trap
“Selecting a trap shows its readings and why it crossed a demo threshold. We keep uncertain records visible and recommend verification instead of presenting the output as a confirmed diagnosis.”

## 2:25–2:45 — Responsible estimates
“The financial estimate uses simulated loss rates and steam prices. It is a scenario estimate, not a measured saving. Real deployment requires calibrated instruments, trap-specific engineering rules, and validation against confirmed outcomes.”

## 2:45–3:00 — Close
“SteamSage aims to help industrial teams focus inspections, explain why an asset is flagged, and connect maintenance prioritization to transparent cost assumptions.”
